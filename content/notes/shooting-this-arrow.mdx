---
title: "Shooting This Arrow"
date: "2026-10-20"
published: true
---

I'm an arrow. Just flying.

Shot by my past self.

I don't know where I'm going. Neither did he. But he saw something.

And he decided to shoot.

My path will be decided by the resistance of the future. I'm sharp enough. I have faith in him.

For whatever he was, I trust his judgments fully. He didn't know how, but he knew where to aim.

And when I fall, I'll be shot again. And again. Each flight carries the experience of the one before it.

He gave me faith, I gave him understanding.

> For the weight I carry, the beauty I find, and the man I choose to become.
